01ABOUT

A generalist by design

I graduated from UNSW with a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Aerospace Engineering, Class 2:1, Distinction WAM. My thesis investigated supersonic wake dynamics behind a bluff body at Mach 3, using CFD and MATLAB to understand how the flow developed and why it behaved the way it did.

I am naturally curious about how things work, and I've ended up exploring that through aircraft, CAD, CFD, manufacturing and hands-on troubleshooting. I've gone from producing fabrication drawings at Metwest Steel, to designing aircraft cabin modifications at Qantas, to fault-finding precision equipment at Noritsu Koki. I enjoy moving between the computer and the physical thing. Modelling something, building or working with it, testing it, and then figuring out what went wrong when reality doesn't quite match the model.

Supersonic Bluff Body Wake Dynamics

High-fidelity compressible-flow CFD of a bluff body wake at Mach 3, with structured mesh refinement and spectral/statistical post-processing in MATLAB. AERO4110 DESIGN PART 23

Kit-Plane Aircraft Design

Full-aircraft design study for a two-seat kit-plane. The project was guided by the Request for Proposal (RFP), which specified stringent mission requirements including range, endurance at various altitudes, weather adaptability and take-off and landing parameters. Over the past ten weeks, the team at Wombat Aerospace has developed a bush plane optimised for long and demanding missions in harsh conditions. The design emphasises structural strength, fuel efficiency and ease of maintenance, while offering unseen benefits at a competitive price. The Kingfisher 210 is intended to provide an affordable, reliable and high-performing aircraft for Australian farmers – the backbone of the nation’s economy – and recreational pilots seeking similar advantages.

Role within the Project

Led the design and analysis of aircraft Weight & Balance, Stability, internal layout, fuselage and tail wing AOA. Developed flight characteristic simulations and assessed compliance with CASA, FAA Part 23 and EASA standards. Used XFOIL, XFLR5, Flow5 and MATLAB for lift, drag and stability analysis, while applying anthropometric and ergonomic data to optimise the internal layout.
