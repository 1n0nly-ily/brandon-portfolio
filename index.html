01ABOUT

A generalist by design

I graduated from UNSW with a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Aerospace Engineering, Class 2:1, Distinction WAM. My thesis modelled supersonic wake dynamics behind a bluff body at Mach 3, and it taught me the lesson every job since has reinforced: a result is only as good as the process that produced it, and only as useful as the report that explains it.

I move comfortably between CAD systems, CFD solvers, and a multimeter, and I'd rather understand a system fully — before and after I touch it — than treat any tool as a black box. I'm currently expanding that range at Noritsu Koki, where fault-finding is a daily exercise in narrowing possibility spaces down to root cause.
