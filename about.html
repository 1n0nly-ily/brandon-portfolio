A generalist by design

I graduated from UNSW with a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Aerospace Engineering, Class 2:1, Distinction WAM. My thesis investigated supersonic wake dynamics behind a bluff body at Mach 3, using CFD and MATLAB to understand how the flow developed and why it behaved the way it did.

I'm naturally curious about how things work, and I've ended up exploring that through aircraft, CAD, CFD, manufacturing and hands-on troubleshooting. I've gone from producing fabrication drawings at Metwest Steel, to designing aircraft cabin modifications at Qantas, to fault-finding precision equipment at Noritsu Koki. I enjoy moving between the computer and the physical thing — modelling something, building or working with it, testing it, and then figuring out what went wrong when reality doesn't quite match the model.
